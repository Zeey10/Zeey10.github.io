GIS-aided hydrologic design for flood mitigation

Final year project, Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife. Supervised by Surv. Dr. C. Oluwadare.

Problem
Parts of the campus flood after heavy rain. The project covered Road 1 and the major academic areas.
Data
ALOS 12.5 m DEM, NiMet rainfall records, and a field inventory of existing drainage.
Method
  1. Processed the DEM and ran terrain analysis.
  2. Derived flow direction and flow accumulation, then delineated watersheds.
  3. Mapped the existing drainage and identified flood-prone areas.
  4. Assessed runoff and combined it with field data to design a sustainable drainage network.
